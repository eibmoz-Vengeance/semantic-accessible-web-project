Contact

This project is at an early stage.

No public contact channel yet

We are currently focusing on learning, testing ideas, and improving this small demonstration. We have not set up a public project email address or other contact channel, so this page does not collect messages or publish personal contact details.

We hope to add a suitable way to get in touch if the project develops to a point where we have work to share and a clear way to communicate about it. At that time, we may also explore collaboration or making parts of the project available as open source.

A shared challenge

In the meantime, we encourage developers, students, and curious builders to explore ways to make websites and applications easier to use—including for people who rely on assistive technologies. Better experiences are a shared goal, and there is room for many people to contribute ideas and solutions.

This page is intentionally simple and may be updated as the project evolves.
